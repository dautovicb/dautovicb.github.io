How can I help?

For help with any of my apps or games, including Bake It Big! and Consciousness: Dimensions, please email me. You can report a problem, ask a question, or share feedback and suggestions.

bdautovic2@etf.unsa.ba

Please include the app or game name in your subject line so I can identify your request.

Reporting a problem

To help me understand what happened, include:

  • The app or game name and version, if available.
  • Your device model and operating system version.
  • A short description of the problem and the steps that led to it.
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if it helps explain the issue.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information. Remove personal information from screenshots before sending them.

Before you get in touch

Try closing and reopening the app, restarting your device, and checking for an app update.

If a game stores progress locally, deleting the app can remove your saved progress. Please contact me before reinstalling if you are unsure whether your progress can be recovered.

Purchase questions

If your question concerns an in-app purchase in an app that offers them, include the app name, the item purchased, and a description of the issue. Do not send your Apple Account password or payment details.

Purchase options vary by app. Bake It Big! does not currently offer in-app purchases.

Privacy questions

You can use the same email address for privacy questions or requests. Please include the relevant app or game name.
