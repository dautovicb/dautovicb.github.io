Bake It Big! · Apple iOS

Privacy Policy

Developed by Beriz Dautović

Effective Date: November 8, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how information may be processed when you use Bake It Big!, a bakery management and idle tycoon game for Apple iOS developed by Beriz Dautović (“the developer”).

The game does not currently include advertisements or in-app purchases.

This policy applies to the game and its integrated services. It does not describe the privacy practices of the developer’s portfolio website or Apple’s services generally.

2. Information We Collect

Local game data. The game stores information locally on your device to save your progress and support gameplay. This includes virtual currency, bakery and location progress, unlocks, upgrades, objectives, tutorial status, audio preferences, and timing information used to calculate earnings while you are away.

Technical information. The game is built with Unity. Unity and its service components may process technical information automatically, such as device and application information, identifiers, and diagnostic data, depending on the services enabled. Unity Analytics and legacy crash reporting are disabled in the current game configuration.

Support correspondence. If you email the developer, your message includes your email address and any information you choose to provide. This information is used to respond to your questions and handle support or privacy requests. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request.

3. Advertising and In-App Purchases

The game does not currently display advertisements, use advertising SDKs, or offer in-app purchases. Virtual currency and gameplay unlocks are part of the game’s progression system.

Advertising may be introduced in a future version. If advertising or in-app purchases are added, this policy will be updated to describe the services involved, the information they process, and any available privacy choices. Any required permissions or consent will be requested before the relevant processing begins.

4. Third-Party Services

Apple. Apple distributes the game through the App Store and processes information under its own policies. See the Apple Privacy Policy and App Store & Privacy.

Unity. The game uses the Unity engine and its service components. Information processed by Unity is subject to its Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

The game does not require an account and does not operate a custom backend or cloud-save service.

5. Data Storage, Sharing, and Security

Game progress and settings are stored locally using Unity PlayerPrefs, including a backup of the saved progress. This local storage is not encrypted by the game. Progress is saved periodically and when the app pauses or closes.

Local game data remains on your device until it is removed or replaced. Device backups may retain this information according to your iOS and backup settings.

The game does not upload your saved gameplay progress to a developer-operated server. Information processed through Apple or Unity is handled under their respective privacy practices. Deleting local game data does not automatically delete information held by these providers.

Support correspondence may be retained to respond to your request and address related issues. You can request deletion using the contact details below, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. The developer cannot guarantee absolute security of information stored on your device or processed by third-party services.

6. Children's Privacy

The game does not ask players to provide their name, email address, or other contact details to play. Third-party services may process technical information as described in this policy.

Parents or guardians who have questions or concerns about information relating to a child may contact the developer using the details below.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and the information involved, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or portability of your personal information, restrict or object to processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

You can remove local game data by deleting the app from your device. Restoring a device backup may restore saved data. For requests concerning information held by the developer, use the contact details below. Apple and Unity also provide privacy request channels under their respective policies.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated as the game or its privacy practices change. The current version will be available on this page with an updated effective date. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law.
